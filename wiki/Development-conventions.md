# Development conventions

Operational conventions used throughout the repo.

## General

- Prefer whole-file “drop-in” changes when updating templates or widely used files.
- Keep docs in English.
- Keep math exposition in `docs/` and operational workflow in `wiki/`.

## Python

- Prefer clean, typed code.
- Keep docstrings consistent and informative.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies in core utilities.

## Docs

- MyST/Sphinx build must be clean.
- Use citations consistently and avoid duplicate citation keys.
- Keep background pages short and link out to references.
